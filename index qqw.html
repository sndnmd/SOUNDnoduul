VOICE 1 — 4 LFOs
An LFO periodically modulates one parameter of a voice - pitch for vibrato, cutoff for a wobble, volume for tremolo. Each voice has 4 independent LFOs, each with its own target, depth, and rate (fixed Hz or synced to the beat). S&H (sample & hold) adds a 5th, stepped-random waveform. RETRIG resets the phase at every note instead of running free; FADE IN ramps the depth in over time after each note starts, independent of RETRIG. PHASE shifts the waveform's starting point (wraps past 0°/360°, DOUBLE-TAP resets to 0). UNIPOLAR keeps modulation one-directional (0..DEPTH) instead of centered (-DEPTH..+DEPTH), same peak either way. The small graph on each card shows the live waveform shape and current phase.

PERFORMANCE FX (halten)
HOLD for the effect, release to return to normal. DOUBLE-TAP any button to latch it engaged for the rest of the pattern (releases at the next 16-step boundary). TAP an empty slot to save everything here - turns green. TAP a saved slot to load it and enter EDIT! mode. TAP again to arm SAVE?. TAP once more to save back into the slot - green again. DOUBLE-TAP a saved slot to load it cleanly, straight to green. HOLD a saved slot to arm DELETE? - tap again to confirm. A slot also remembers which PERFORMANCE FX were latched (and which variant/color each was in) when saved - recalling it re-engages exactly that combination. While EDIT! is active, double-tap+swipe an FX button to arm-then-commit whichever variant you land on (instead of only ever latching the plain/red state) - or hold the LATCH button and tap+swipe any FX for the same result without needing a double-tap. Loading a slot without playback running, or while in EDIT!, shows latched FX as a colored border rather than fully engaging them - only real playback (or holding an FX yourself) fills the button in.
MACRO CHAIN
Same as SEQ: TAP CHAIN MODE, then tap saved macro slots to build the sequence - tap the same slot again to increase its repeat count. Advances after SEQ LENGTH steps per repeat. START/STOP controls playback separately from building. Each segment's latched PERFORMANCE FX (if any) engage for real during playback, staying constant through repeats and only changing when a different slot's segment comes up.
